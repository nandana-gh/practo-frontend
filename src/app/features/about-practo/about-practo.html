Quality Healthcare Made Simple

Our Mission

Practo is on a mission to make quality healthcare affordable and accessible for over a billion+ Indians. We believe in empowering our users with the most accurate, comprehensive, and curated information and care, enabling them to make better healthcare decisions.

Health is a habit

It is the journey that takes you to new destinations every day with endless possibilities of life on the back of happiness, energy, and hope. Practo wants to make this journey easy for every Indian and help them live a healthier and longer life.

Our offerings

  • Comprehensive medical directory with detailed, verified information about more than a lakh doctor partners across the country
  • Online appointment booking at over 9,000 clinics and hospitals with doctors who use Practo Prime
  • Online consultation with trusted doctors across 20+ specialties
  • Plus, subscription-based health plans, that provide unlimited online consultations* with doctors 24*7*365
    *T&C apply. Consultations are subject to fair usage policy
  • Ray, Practo's award-winning practice management software, which is used by 10,000+ clinics
  • Insta, a full-stack HIMS solution, which is trusted by 500+ clients across 1,200+ facilities
  • Diagnostic Tests through Practo Associate Labs to get samples collected from the comfort and safety of one's home
  • Medicine delivery by a network of verified pharmacies across the country

Our approach to healthcare

Providing high-quality, trusted, and accessible care to patients

Connect

Trust
