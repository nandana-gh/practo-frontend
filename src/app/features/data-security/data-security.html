Secure Server Infrastructure

We are ISO 27001
certified

ISO 27001 is a globally recognized independent international security standard. This means that a third party has evaluated the processes that we have in place to secure your data and have certified us as adhering to international standards.

  • 24x7 security personnel guarding the data centers where your data resides
  • Biometric access for all personnel at our data centers
  • Strong encryption methods and strict access control to databases over a private network

Data privacy for patients

  • Your health records, your rules.
  • You alone have access to your health records. Only the doctors you consult can view them.
  • You can selectively share your records with a doctor or lab on Practo.
  • You can revoke access to your records at any time.

Data privacy for doctors

  • We will not share your data with any third party, without your consent.
  • Data access is strictly based on the roles that you define. You are in complete control of your data.
  • Practo administrators do not have access to your patient or practice data.

Strict access control over your patient & clinic data

Every practice is isolated and access is restricted exclusively to authenticated users added to the practice. There are strict role-based access rules to govern data access. E.g. A receptionist cannot see patient health records without the doctor's explicit permission.

Your data is safe, globally

We deploy strict security measures across all our data centers, regardless of geography. Our security framework ensures compliance with data protection laws wherever we operate.

Continuous data backups

We automatically backup your data every 30 mins to multiple secure, geographically distributed locations. In case of an unexpected event, your data is restored instantly with no loss.

Advanced monitoring and incident response

We have automated tracking systems that log every access attempt. Any suspicious behavior triggers immediate alerts to our 24x7 security operations team.

your health data

Data Security at Rest

All sensitive health data stored in our databases is protected with AES-256 encryption. Our infrastructure ensures that even direct access to the database yields only encrypted, unreadable text.

Strictly governed access

Only you and the healthcare professionals you authorize can access your data. We implement rigorous internal policies preventing any Practo employee from accessing patient health records.

Two-factor authentication

We provide two-factor authentication (2FA) for your account. Even if someone obtains your password, they cannot access your health records without the OTP sent to your registered device.

Regular security audits

We conduct independent security audits and penetration tests regularly to identify and patch potential vulnerabilities, ensuring our systems remain impenetrable.
